# Pricing & legal assumptions (from "Założenia dotyczące zakupu energii elektrycznej przez MS AGH.pdf")
Campus = large customer, tariff group B (MV), Tauron Dystrybucja. Energy bought at exchange-linked prices. All NET, ignore VAT.

| component | PLN/kWh | when |
|---|---|---|
| energy | RDN spot + ~0.05 margin | varies per 15min/h |
| capacity fee (opłata mocowa) | 0.2194 | workdays 07:00–21:59 only |
| variable distribution B23 | 0.05–0.10 by zone → may simplify to flat 0.08 | always |
| quality fee | 0.0332 | always |
| OZE + cogeneration + excise | ~0.015 | always |

- Grid export (surplus): sold at PSE RCE price of that period, no extra fees.
- Inter-building flows WITHOUT cooperative allowed within common connection / internal AGH network → behind-the-meter self-consumption avoids full unit price.

## Energy cooperative (spółdzielnia energetyczna)
- Energy produced in coop installations and used by members: exempt from OZE, capacity, cogeneration fees. Excise exempt only if total coop RES capacity ≤ 1 MW.
- AGH incl. dorms/hala/basen = ONE legal entity = one member; coop needs several members.
- From 2026: new coop members must be able to produce ≥70% of their energy need.
- Surplus settled volumetrically 1:0.6; variable distribution only partially exempt.
→ Coop is a feasibility risk; any coop scenario must address members + 70% rule.
